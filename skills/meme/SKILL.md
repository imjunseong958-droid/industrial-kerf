# Meme edits

- Timing is the joke. Cut on the beat, the word, or the reaction you
  observed; never claim a beat or a line you did not see or hear.
- Prefer one or two clear moves per beat: a hard cut, a speed change, a
  punch-in, a freeze. Stacking effects muddies the gag.
- A faster setup and a slower punchline is an option, not a preset. Keep
  speech intelligible unless distortion is the joke.
- For a punch-in, isolate the moment and scale or crop in on it, then return
  to the original framing unless the user wants it held.
- Leave a beat after the punchline before the cut out.
- Look at the result around every change before handing it over.
