What this app carries, and on what terms. SOURCE.txt says where the source of all of
it is.

Industrial Kerf's own code
  MIT (LICENSE.txt). The app as a whole is under the GNU GPL version 3, as it joins
  that code with Homebrew's GPL build of FFmpeg below (COPYING-GPLv3.txt).

FFmpeg (Contents/MacOS/ffmpeg and ffprobe, and the libav*, libsw* dylibs in
Contents/Frameworks)
  Homebrew's build, GPL version 3 with libx264 and the other libraries it is built
  with. FFmpeg-BUILD.txt lists how it was configured.

Rust crates (compiled into Contents/MacOS/kerf-editor, kerfd, kerf and kerf-mcp)
  The programs are written in Rust and statically link the crates their Cargo.lock
  names, among them GPUI (Apache-2.0), which draws the editor. Each crate's licence,
  most of them MIT or Apache-2.0, is in its source, in the vendor folder of the source
  archive (SOURCE.txt).

The other libraries in Contents/Frameworks
  Each from a Homebrew formula, on its own licence, listed below.

Inter, JetBrains Mono, Pretendard, IBM Plex, Noto Serif and Nanum Myeongjo (compiled into kerf-editor)
  SIL OFL 1.1. See fonts/.

Code from ArtCraft's EffectCraft and FilmCraft, and the fonts of its text engine
  See licenses/NOTICE.
